# Separate Cloud contributor agreement

Community contributions are accepted under the open-source license assigned to
their directory and the Developer Certificate of Origin process described in
`CONTRIBUTING.md`.

ChartViz Cloud does not automatically copy Community server contributions. If a
specific contribution is useful in separately licensed Cloud code, maintainers
may contact its author and offer a separate contributor agreement granting the
additional rights required for that use. The agreement must identify the exact
contribution, rights, parties, and effective date and must be accepted before
the code enters Cloud.

No contributor is required to accept that separate agreement. Declining it
does not revoke the original open-source contribution or prevent the Community
project from accepting an otherwise eligible change.
