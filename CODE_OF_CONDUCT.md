# Code of Conduct

ChartViz Community is committed to a respectful, harassment-free project for
everyone. Be constructive, assume good intent while verifying technical
claims, respect privacy, and focus criticism on ideas and code rather than
people.

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, deliberate disclosure of private
information, and sustained disruption are not acceptable in project spaces.
Maintainers may edit or remove content and temporarily or permanently restrict
participation when needed to protect the community.

Report conduct concerns privately to `support@chartviz.xyz`. Include links and
context, but do not forward unrelated personal data. Maintainers will review
reports confidentially, disclose information only as necessary to investigate,
and avoid assigning a reporter to investigate a complaint involving themselves.

This policy applies to repositories, issues, reviews, community channels, and
public representation of the project.
